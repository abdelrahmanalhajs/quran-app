## English (secondary)

**Title** (21 chars)
Quran - القرآن الكريم

**Short description** (79 chars)
Read the Quran in Uthmani script, translation & tafsir, audio by 7 reciters

**Full description**

A complete companion for reading, listening to, and reflecting on the Holy Quran — built to be your daily reference for recitation, study, and remembrance.

📖 THE QURAN
- Full Uthmani-script text, rendered page-by-page exactly like a printed Mushaf, or as a separate ayah-by-ayah list
- 3 font sizes (Small, Medium, Large) with two-finger pinch-to-zoom on the page
- Sajda (prostration) marks and Hizb/quarter-Hizb markers placed automatically and accurately
- Long-press any verse to instantly see its translation and tafsir (commentary)
- Automatically resumes exactly where you left off

🎧 AUDIO RECITATION
- Recitation by 7 renowned reciters
- Background playback with lock-screen controls

🕋 PRAYER TIMES & QIBLAH
- Accurate prayer times based on your location
- Interactive Qiblah compass

🤲 ATHKAR & DUAA
- Morning and evening athkar, post-prayer athkar, before-sleep athkar, and Friday (Jumu'ah) athkar
- Daily and weekly reminders so you never miss them

📿 DAILY HADITH
- A new hadith every day, delivered with a notification

Supports both light and dark themes, and works offline for reading once content is downloaded.
