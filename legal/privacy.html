Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-24

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Quran - القرآن الكريم" mobile app ("the app") handles your information. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer, not a company.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not require account creation or sign-in. It does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifiers to the developer. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Location

If you use the Prayer Times or Qiblah features, the app requests your device's location (while in use) so it can calculate prayer times and the qiblah direction for your current position. This location is sent only to the public prayer-times API (aladhan.com) needed to perform that calculation, is not stored by the developer, and is not linked to any identity. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; the Prayer Times and Qiblah tabs simply won't work without it.

Notifications

If you enable daily hadith, hourly zikr, sleep athkar, Jumu'ah athkar, or prayer-time reminder notifications, these are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using your device's local notification system. No notification content or schedule is sent to or stored by the developer.

Internet access

The app connects to the following public, third-party content APIs to download Quran text, translations, tafsir, and prayer times. No account or personal data is sent to them beyond the location described above:

  • api.alquran.cloud — Quran text and translation
  • api.qurancdn.com — tafsir (commentary)
  • api.aladhan.com — prayer times and qiblah direction
  • Reciter audio is streamed from each reciter's publicly hosted recitation server.

Downloaded Quran text and audio may be cached locally on your device purely to reduce repeat downloads and allow offline reading; this cache stays on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
